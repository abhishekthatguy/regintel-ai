# Email Configuration

## Standard mailbox
Every employee gets a firstname.lastname@company.com mailbox on day one via automated provisioning.

## Shared mailboxes
Team shared mailboxes require an access ticket naming the mailbox and business justification. Access is granted within 1 business day.

## Mobile access
Email on personal phones requires enrollment in the mobile device management profile. Jailbroken devices are blocked.

## Quotas and archiving
Mailboxes have a 100 GB quota. Messages older than 2 years auto-archive to the online archive, which is searchable but slower.
