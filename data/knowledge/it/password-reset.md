# Password Reset

Self-service password reset is available at the identity portal for all employees.

## Reset your password
1. Go to the identity portal and choose "Forgot password".
2. Verify with your registered MFA method.
3. Set a new password (minimum 12 characters, no reuse of last 5).

## Account locked
Accounts lock after 5 failed attempts. Wait 15 minutes or contact the IT helpdesk. Repeated lockouts may indicate a compromised account — report it.
