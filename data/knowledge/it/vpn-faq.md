# VPN FAQ

## Is VPN required for email?
No. Email, chat, and most SaaS tools work without VPN. VPN is required for intranet sites, file shares, and internal admin tools.

## Does VPN work on phones?
The SecureLink mobile app supports iOS and Android for intranet access only.

## Can I use VPN abroad?
Yes, except in embargoed regions where connections are geo-blocked for compliance.

## Why does VPN slow my connection?
All corporate traffic routes through the nearest gateway; split tunneling keeps SaaS traffic direct to reduce latency.
