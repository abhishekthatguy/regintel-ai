# Reporting Phishing

Use the "Report Phishing" button in the mail client — this is the fastest path.

## If you clicked a link
1. Do not delete the email.
2. Report it immediately via the phishing button or security mailbox.
3. Change your password if you entered credentials.

## Response SLA
The security team triages reports within 4 business hours. Confirmed incidents trigger a forced password reset.
