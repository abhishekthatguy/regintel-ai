# Laptop and Hardware Policy

Standard issue: one laptop per employee, refreshed every 3 years.

## Repairs
Report hardware faults via an IT ticket. Do not attempt self-repair; unauthorized repair voids warranty.

## Loaners
A loaner laptop is issued while yours is under repair, subject to availability. Loaners must be returned within 5 days of repair completion.

## Peripherals
Monitors, keyboards and headsets are requestable via a hardware ticket with manager approval.
