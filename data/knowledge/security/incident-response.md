# Incident Response (Security team internal)

## Severity classification
SEV1: active data exfiltration or system compromise. SEV2: contained malware or credential exposure. SEV3: policy violations without confirmed compromise.

## Response SLAs
SEV1: engage the incident commander within 30 minutes, 24/7. SEV2: triage within 4 business hours. SEV3: review within 3 business days.

## Evidence handling
Preserve affected systems unmodified. Chain of custody is recorded for anything that may become legal evidence.

## Communications
Only the communications lead may discuss incidents externally. Employees must not disclose incident details on social channels.
