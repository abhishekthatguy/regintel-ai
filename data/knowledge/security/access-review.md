# Access Reviews

## Cadence
Access to sensitive systems is reviewed quarterly. Managers certify their team's access within 10 business days of the review opening.

## Scope
Reviews cover privileged systems, financial tools, and any system containing personal data.

## Revocation
Uncertified access is automatically revoked at review close and can be re-requested through the normal access ticket process.
