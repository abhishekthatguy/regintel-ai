# Expense Reimbursement

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date.

## Requirements
Itemized receipts are mandatory above a small threshold. Manager approval is required for all claims.

## Timeline
Approved expenses reimburse in the next payroll cycle. Rejected claims include a reason and can be resubmitted once corrected.
