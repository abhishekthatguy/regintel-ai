# Performance Reviews (HR internal)

## Cycle
Annual review cycle runs October through December. Mid-year check-ins are recommended but not mandatory.

## Calibration
Ratings are calibrated per department by the HR partners before release. Individual ratings are confidential to employee, manager, and HR.

## Appeals
Employees may appeal a rating within 10 business days of release via the HR partner for their department.
