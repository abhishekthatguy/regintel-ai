# Benefits

Core benefits: health insurance (employee + 2 dependents), life insurance, retirement contribution match up to 6%.

## Wellness
Annual wellness allowance of a fixed amount, claimable via the benefits portal with receipts.

## Enrollment window
Benefits changes are allowed during the annual enrollment window or after qualifying life events (marriage, childbirth).
