# Payroll

## Pay dates
Salary is paid on the last business day of each month. Bank transfers may take 1-2 days to appear depending on your bank.

## Payslips
Payslips publish to the HRMS 2 days before payday. Tax withholding and deductions are itemized per statutory rules.

## Corrections
Report pay discrepancies to payroll within 5 business days of payday. Corrections are processed in the next cycle or as an off-cycle run for material errors.
