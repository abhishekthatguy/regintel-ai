# Code of Conduct

## Core principles
Act with integrity, respect colleagues, protect company information, and avoid conflicts of interest.

## Reporting concerns
Concerns can be raised to your manager, HR partner, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

## Acknowledgement
All employees must complete the annual conduct acknowledgement and training within 30 days of the annual window opening.
