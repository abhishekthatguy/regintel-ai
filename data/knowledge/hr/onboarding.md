# Onboarding

Day 1: badge + laptop issued, Entra ID account activated, benefits enrollment link sent.

## First week
Complete mandatory compliance training (security, code of conduct) within 7 days.

## Buddy program
Every new hire is assigned a department buddy for the first 30 days.
