# Parental Leave

## Entitlement
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks paid leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave can be split into up to 3 blocks within 12 months of the event.

## Notice
Provide at least 4 weeks notice where possible via the HRMS parental leave workflow.

## Benefits continuation
All benefits continue during approved parental leave.
