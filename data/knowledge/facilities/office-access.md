# Office Access

## Badges
Badges are issued on day one and required at all entry points. Lost badges are replaced for a small fee after reporting to facilities.

## Visitors
All visitors must be pre-registered in the visitor system and escorted in controlled areas.

## After-hours access
Standard badge access covers 7:00-20:00 on business days. After-hours access requires manager approval registered with facilities.
