# Emergency Procedures

## Evacuation
On alarm, leave via the nearest marked exit and proceed to the assembly point in the north parking area. Do not use elevators.

## Floor wardens
Each floor has designated wardens in high-visibility vests who sweep and report to the incident coordinator.

## Drills
Evacuation drills run twice yearly. Attendance is tracked and reported to facilities compliance.
