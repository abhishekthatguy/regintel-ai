# Ergonomics

## Assessments
Free ergonomic assessments are bookable through the facilities portal. Recommendations are implemented within 10 business days.

## Equipment
Adjustable chairs and monitor arms are standard. Sit-stand desks require an ergonomic assessment or medical note.

## Home office
Remote employees may claim a one-time home office stipend for approved ergonomic equipment, claimed through the finance portal.
