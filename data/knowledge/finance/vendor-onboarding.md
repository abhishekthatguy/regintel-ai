# Vendor Onboarding

## New vendors
New vendors require a completed onboarding form, tax documentation, and a signed agreement before any purchase order is issued.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are net 30 from invoice receipt. Early-payment discounts are applied where negotiated.

## Invoice submission
Vendors submit invoices to the AP portal referencing a valid PO number. Invoices without a PO are returned unpaid.
