# Corporate Cards

## Eligibility
Employees with regular travel or procurement duties may request a corporate card with manager approval.

## Usage rules
Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Personal use is prohibited even if repaid. Cash advances are disabled.

## Reconciliation
Card transactions must be reconciled in the finance portal within 14 days of statement close with itemized receipts.

## Lost or stolen cards
Report immediately to the card issuer hotline and finance operations; fraudulent charges are covered when reported within 24 hours.
