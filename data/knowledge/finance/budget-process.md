# Annual Budget Process (Finance internal)

## Timeline
Budget planning opens in August; department submissions close in October; board approval in December.

## Templates
Departments submit using the standard template covering headcount, opex, and capex with quarterly phasing.

## Variance
Actuals vs budget are reviewed monthly. Variances over 5% require written commentary from the budget owner.
