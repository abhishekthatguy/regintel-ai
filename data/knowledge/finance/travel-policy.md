# Business Travel

## Booking
All travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 7 days in advance for domestic trips and 14 days for international.

## Class of service
Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for 6-10 hours; business class requires director approval regardless of duration.

## Per diem
Daily meal allowances are set per city tier and published in the finance portal. Per diem requires no receipts.

## Approval
Travel over the estimated budget threshold requires pre-approval from the cost-center owner before booking.
